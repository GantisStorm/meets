Scope: The Meetings browser in the native macOS dashboard — "All Meetings" and every folder scope, including the dated sections and the follow-up threads hanging inside them, the folder level above them, and the browser header with its meeting actions and display controls.

Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: People reviewing a growing archive of recorded meetings need to find a meeting, recognize which meetings belong to the same follow-up thread, and open the right one — without the archive turning into an undifferentiated list.

Primary task: Scan the ledger for the thread that matters, open a meeting, and move, delete, or start a follow-up from the exact meeting the user is looking at.

Constraints: Preserve opening, move/create-folder, delete confirmation, follow-up creation, and status/folder context. Keep Calendar/Coming Up and active-recording behavior untouched. Native SwiftUI controls, macOS 14.2, light and dark appearances, and a detail column that can narrow to about 220 points once the sidebar is subtracted. No transcript loading for the whole library and no per-row database reads, and no per-row date-formatter allocation.

Chosen direction: A list of meetings by day, in text and spacing only. The page keeps one heading — the scope name — and under it the scope's own navigation: in a folder scope the breadcrumb ("All Meetings › Clients") in caption size and the secondary tone, then one line of child folders as plain text buttons ("Clients 24", the count in the tertiary tone, 20 points apart, stacking into a column when the line runs out). Nothing beside the rows is filled, bordered, or badged: no folder tiles, no count line, no chips, no pills, no rules.

Each section is a `LazyVStack` with pinned headers: the section title alone — "Today", "Yesterday", "Earlier this week", "Last week", then month names with the year only when it is not this year — in sentence case at caption size in the secondary tone, filled with the base canvas so rows scroll under it. Under it the archive is one flat column of rows, and row order is the ledger's spine: `MeetingBrowserLogic.flatRows` takes every node out of the follow-up shelves — roots and descendants alike — and stable-sorts them by their own start time, so a follow-up is filed under the day it happened rather than under the meeting it follows on from, and `ledgerGroups` cuts that list into sections, each title appearing once because consecutive rows in a time-sorted list can never return to a kind they have left. Ties keep the order the shelves gave them, which is thread order, and a row whose timestamp does not parse sorts last either way.

A row is one line, and nothing is drawn around it but the hover: `HStack(alignment: .firstTextBaseline, spacing: 12)` holding a right-aligned 84-point time gutter (the time alone under Today and Yesterday, "Wed 9:30 AM" under the named weeks, "17 · 9:00 AM" under a month), the title at 14pt regular in the primary tone on one line, a `Spacer`, the duration in the trailing corner ("46m"), and the existing ellipsis menu. The row is one plain `Button`, so the whole line opens the meeting and stays reachable from the keyboard; the menu is its sibling, never its child, and sits at full opacity as a bare ellipsis, tertiary at rest and primary under the pointer. Row padding is 8 by 7 with no gap between rows, and the hover fill is the only surface the browser has. A meeting that is not completed says so in a word in the status colour beside the title — the recording red while it is live, the transcribing amber while its notes are still being written — and a follow-up is marked rather than indented: a 10-point arrow glyph before the title, with "Follow-up to <title>" on hover and in the accessibility label, so nothing is ever nested and no row grows past its lines. While a date range is active, a row the range excluded is drawn at 0.55 opacity and explains itself in the same tooltip: it is on screen to keep a follow-up thread intact.

The toolbar is one right-aligned row of plain text — a "New" menu holding Quick Note and Import Audio, the range menu showing its own label ("All time ▾"), and the sort as a one-click text toggle between "Newest first" and "Oldest first" — and it stacks at the trailing edge once the row cannot hold all three rather than clipping. The empty state is one caption line — "No meetings yet.", "No meetings in this folder.", "No meetings in this range." — and no card.

Memorable interaction: Hover is the only surface, and the only thing that changes. The ellipsis sits in the tertiary tone at rest and turns primary under the pointer while the row takes the hover fill; the follow-up arrow names the meeting it hangs from in a tooltip, and a row kept on screen for thread context says why in the same place. Nothing else moves: the list is text on the canvas, and a follow-up thread is legible without ever being opened — its members are already rows, in date order, each marked with its own arrow.

Review evidence: An offscreen NSHostingView harness at `/private/tmp/meets-shelves-harness` renders the real production views — `MeetingLedgerView` with its section headers and rows, the shared row pieces from `MeetingListItemView`, the extracted `MeetingBrowserHeader` — inside fixed window viewports (1100×800, 280×800, 220×800) scrolled like the product, with synthetic fixtures spanning today, yesterday, earlier this week, last week, two months, and a prior year, in light and dark. Scenarios: `browser` at 1100, `folder` inside a folder scope with its breadcrumb and child line, `filtered` under a one-week range with a retained out-of-range root, and `narrow-280`/`narrow-220`, each in both appearances. The renders confirmed that rows are single lines at 1100 with the time column complete and right-aligned across every section ("10:00 AM", "Thu 10:15 AM", "Wed 11:20 AM", "17 · 9:00 AM" all render whole), that follow-up rows carry the arrow before the title while roots carry none, that the folder line reads "Clients 24 · Product notes 6 · Hiring loops 3 · Archive 0" and the toolbar "New ▾ / All time ▾ / Newest first", that the toolbar's range label follows the active range ("Last week" in the filtered scenario), that a retained out-of-range root in the filtered scenario renders faded against its in-range neighbours, and that nothing overlaps or runs off an edge at any of the three widths. Two numbers were measured rather than guessed. The gutter is 84 points, not 64: at 64 the caption-size "Thu 2:05 PM" truncated to "Thu 2:05…" in the first render, and the widest label a section can ask for ("Wed 11:20 AM") measures 80.1 points. And at 220 points — 188 points of row once the page gutters are taken — the row cannot hold the date, the duration, and a title at once: with the date and the duration fixed, the title collapsed to about 7 points and rendered as nothing, so a compact row tries the date beside the title first, then drops the date, then the duration, and never the title; the date the section heading no longer names is the first thing to go. Hover fill, the tertiary-to-primary transitions, the menus themselves, and pinned-header behaviour while scrolling are present in the layout but are not exercised by a static render, so they stay unverified here. Revised from the ledger pass: the follow-up pill and its unfoldable sublist, the 24-point child indent, the folder chip, the source and retained-recording glyphs, the status badge, the "Outside range" chip, the preview line, the count line, the folder tiles, and the section rules are all gone; a follow-up is a row of its own, and the browser is text, the time column, and the hover.
